The document probably lists people by name.
a.6: KU-RO totals the 10 (personnel?) in lines a.1-5
a.6/7: the rule there apparently introduces a second section on side a, headed by SA-TA (cf. HT 42.1)
Side b therefore presents a third section headed by QI f -TU-NE, a heading on HT 7b.1 and HT 87.1. MA-KA-RI-TE, QI f -TU-NE and DI-KI-SE appear in HT 87, and in both HT 87 & HT 117, QI f -TU-NE (as heading or part of the heading) and DI-KI-SE (as name in a list) appear in the same paragraph.
SA-TA, QI f -TU-NE, and MA-KA-RI-TE may be regions or people supplying personnel.
